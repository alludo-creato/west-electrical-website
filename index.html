WHY IT MATTERS

Everyday power changes can put appliances under stress.

When voltage rises or drops outside an appliance’s operating range, equipment can behave unpredictably, shut down or suffer damage. Repeated instability is a reason to take a closer look at your home’s electrical protection.

01

Lights changing brightness

Repeated dimming or unusually bright lights can be a sign to investigate.

02

Appliances restarting

Unexpected switching off or restarting may point to an unstable supply.

03

Sensitive equipment at home

Electronics and appliances with motors or control boards may need particular care.

A PRACTICAL STEP FOR YOUR HOME

What does an AVP do?

An Automatic Voltage Protector monitors incoming voltage. Depending on the model and settings, it can disconnect supply when voltage goes beyond its set range, then reconnect after conditions return to an acceptable level.

West Electrical Engineering Technology can discuss your home, the appliances you want to protect and a suitable device rating before installation.
